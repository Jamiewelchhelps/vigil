Bridge watch

Each bridge and dam gets its own normal pattern: its long-term trend, its seasonal swing and how it responds to temperature. A structure is flagged when new measurements leave that pattern. Flags go privately to the owner first, with the evidence. Vigil never says a structure is unsafe; it shows how it is moving.
